Good questions

Know exactly what to expect.

Do I need an account or password to begin?

No. The free setup wizard lets you choose platforms, write your post, select a schedule, and review the plan before any login. Your draft is stored on this device, not on a customer account. Create an account or sign in when you are ready to save the setup to your own workspace.

Can I review my workflow before activating it?

Yes. Choose your topics, destinations, timing and approval preferences, then review the complete plan. Activating supported publishing requires authorized connections and an active subscription. Saving a setup alone never starts publishing.

Do I have to give you my social media password?

No. The customer version will use each supported platform's official connection screens. Platform approvals and eligible account types determine what can be published automatically.

Does it work on every platform right now?

Not yet. Our existing owner-operated publisher supports Facebook Pages, and the mobile web starter can schedule Facebook Page text posts. Other platforms require separately approved publishing integrations.

Can it publish while my phone is off?

Yes, when deployed on an always-on server with a valid authorization, a supported destination, and an active subscription. The hosted scheduler does the work, not your phone.

Can it monitor any website or niche automatically?

Niche templates can support many types of content, but source collection must respect licenses, privacy and platform rules. A URL alone is not authorization to scrape, copy photographs, or publish personal information.

What happens if I stop paying?

Premium publishing should pause on the server. You should still be able to manage billing and retrieve account information. Subscription cancellation itself must remain straightforward.

Can I download the iPhone app now?

The mobile-friendly web app is available for testing; it is not an App Store download. A public iOS App Store release requires production hosting, an iOS application, subscription support and Apple approval.
