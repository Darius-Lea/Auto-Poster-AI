Your information in Auto Poster AI

What this version stores

Account name and email, a password hash, drafts and publishing times, brand and source instructions, authorized Page identifiers and encrypted service connection tokens, subscription status and customer references, delivery records, and available performance observations.

Anonymous setup and idea worksheets are kept in browser storage until you choose to save them to an account. Clearing browser data can remove unsaved work. Stored account data belongs to an authenticated workspace; it is not intentionally shared with other customers.

When information leaves the app

Approved posts go to the social platform you selected. Source automations fetch the authorized feed you provided. When you request managed AI drafting or analysis, the relevant prompt, brand guidance and selected source/report data are sent to a configured AI provider. Availability fallback may use another configured provider. Canva authorization and design operations use Canva. Muse.ai and CapCut links currently open external services; their personal accounts are not synchronized by this version.

Account controls

Use account settings to export supported account data, change your password or delete account/workspace data subject to active billing checks. Deleting the app account does not delete posts already published to Facebook or cancel a subscription with an external provider. Financial references needed for billing reconciliation may be retained without the customer content. Hosting backups and platform retention must also be covered by the operator’s production retention policy.

Do not put private credentials or sensitive customer information in creative prompts or public posts. No advertising-cookie or tracking-platform integration is added by this application release.

Using Auto Poster AI

What you control

You choose the content, sources, business Pages, posting times and approval rules. Only use material and accounts you are authorized to use. Review factual claims, image rights and platform policies before publishing. AI text and suggested marketing plans can be wrong and require review.

What the service offers today

Draft preparation, selected Facebook Page text publishing when connected and authorized, structured-feed/text automations, managed AI assistance when configured, and recorded delivery/performance reports. Some editing or network options are future capabilities or external handoffs. The service does not promise sales, profit, viral reach, platform approval, a finished manufactured product or an online storefront.

Payment and cancellation

Prices, frequency and subscription terms must be shown before any purchase. Saving a draft does not purchase or activate a subscription. Manage an active subscription through the billing portal before deleting the account. Basic permits one posting account per platform and one post per platform per UTC day. A platform rejection or uncertain response may hold a daily allowance to prevent an accidental duplicate.

Availability and delivery

Publishing depends on hosting, account permission, supported platform APIs and subscription status. Connections can expire and third-party services can fail. Check confirmed delivery status instead of assuming that a scheduled item was published. Keep your own copies of important business content.
