I don’t have a Facebook business Page

You can make and save your drafts first. When ready, create a business Page in Facebook, then return here and connect it. Connecting an account lets you choose the exact Page; a personal profile is not a publishing destination.

  1. Open Facebook’s Page creation and follow its instructions.
  2. Return to Connect Facebook Pages.
  3. Choose your business Page, then review its name before scheduling a post.

Facebook account eligibility and permissions apply. If the app says connection is unavailable, the service operator must finish setup; you should not enter developer keys or share your Facebook password here.

When does anything actually post?

  1. Create an account to keep drafts in your own workspace.
  2. Connect a supported business Page.
  3. Review the content and exact posting time.
  4. Activate a supported subscription when that service is available.
  5. Explicitly schedule a post or start an approved automation.

Saving a plan never buys a subscription or starts posting. Basic allows one Page per platform and one post per platform per UTC day. Extra platforms need working authorized connectors; selecting a future platform does not connect it.

A post says “Needs review”

Do not immediately repost: the platform may have accepted a post even if its response was lost. Open your Page and check for the post first. If account access failed, reconnect your Page. An uncertain delivery stays held to avoid duplicates; contact support if the status cannot be resolved.
